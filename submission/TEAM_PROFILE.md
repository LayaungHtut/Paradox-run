## 4. Technical Background & Competition Vision

Team YOUTHs Org entered Digit7 Jam 2026 with a bold ambition: **to build a pure, uncompromising arcade experience natively designed for the open web.** 

Rather than relying on heavy third-party game engines that produce bloated 50 MB+ WebGL packages with long loading spinners, the team set out to prove that modern web standards (Canvas 2D, WebAudio, Svelte 5, and TypeScript) can deliver instantaneous load times (< 0.5s), silky-smooth 60 FPS performance, and deeply engaging cooperative gameplay in a package under 3 MB.

**PARADOX RUN** embodies this vision—a game where every loss teaches the player a lesson, and every past attempt becomes a cooperative teammate.
